Privacy Policy

Context Search Assistant · Last updated: October 2026

Short version: Context Search Assistant does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your notes stay on your device. Your queries go directly to Google's Gemini API.

What is Context Search Assistant?

Context Search Assistant is a browser extension that lets you ask questions and get AI-powered answers on any webpage without leaving it. You can also save notes locally in your browser.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information
  • We do not track your browsing history
  • We do not store your search queries on any server
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties

How your queries are handled

When you ask a question, it is sent directly to Google's Gemini API to generate an answer. This communication happens between your browser and Google's servers. We do not intercept, log, or store these queries. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for how they handle API requests.

Notes storage

Notes you save are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. They never leave your device and are not accessible to us or any third party. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's local storage.

Permissions used

  • activeTab — to inject the search panel on the current page when activated
  • storage — to save your notes locally on your device

Children's privacy

Context Search Assistant does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
